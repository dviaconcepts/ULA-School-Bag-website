# ULA – Uganda Lasting School Bag

Campaign website for ULA, an affordable, government-funded leather school bag for every Ugandan child, developed with SASA College of Industrial Design.

The whole site is one file: `index.html`.

## Turning on online orders
Open `index.html` and find **ORDER SETTINGS** near the bottom:

- `open: true` shows the "Order a bag" section to visitors.
- `whatsapp: "2567XXXXXXXX"` is the sales WhatsApp number, digits only.
- `price: 85000` is the price of one bag in UGX (leave `null` until confirmed).

To preview the hidden order section, add `#order-preview` to the end of the site address.
